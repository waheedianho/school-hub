Academic progress: the average lesson progress, including partially completed lessons. Completing practice does not automatically finish a lesson.

Quiz score: the average of all recorded quiz attempts in this selection. The chart groups these results into school-calendar weeks and leaves weeks without a recorded score blank.

Worksheet completion: completed worksheets divided by all worksheets in the selection.

Portfolio completion: submitted and completed tasks divided by all portfolio tasks. A task needing revision counts again after resubmission.

Topic mastery: the average of the latest quiz score, completed worksheet score, and available portfolio rubric for the topic.

Skill evidence: average tutor rubric marks relative to each criterion’s maximum. It describes assessed portfolio work, including earlier feedback retained after revision.

Mastery bands: Strong: 85–100%; Good: 70–84%; Developing: 60–69%; Needs Support: below 60%. No Score Yet means no assessed evidence is available.

Learning engagement: lessons, quizzes, worksheets, and portfolio tasks with recorded progress or results, divided by the total learning activities.

The page uses the current sample learner records and saved practice on this device. Attendance and learning streaks are not tracked yet.
