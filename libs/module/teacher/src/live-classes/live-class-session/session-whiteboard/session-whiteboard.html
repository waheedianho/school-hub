SENTENCE TYPES

There are four main types of sentences:

1. Simple Sentence – has one independent clause.

Example: The boy runs.

2. Compound Sentence – has two independent clauses joined by a conjunction.

Example: The boy runs, and the girl walks.

3. Complex Sentence – has one independent clause and at least one dependent clause.

Example: Because it rained, we stayed indoors.

4. Compound-Complex Sentence – has two or more independent clauses and at least one dependent clause.

Example: The boy runs, and the girl walks because it is a sunny day.

Examples:

1) Simple: I like reading.

2) Compound: I like reading, but my brother likes football.

3) Complex: When I study hard, I do well in exams.

4) Compound-Complex: I will go to the library, and I will read my book because I want to improve.
